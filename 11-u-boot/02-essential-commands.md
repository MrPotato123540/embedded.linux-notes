- Storage: `mmc`, `fatls`/`fatload`, `ext4ls`/`ext4load`, `sf probe/read/write/erase`, `nand`, `usb`, `ums`

- Booting: `booti`, `bootm`, `bootefi`, `boot`, `pxe`
- Hardware: `i2c`, `gpio`, `fpga load/loadb`, `zynqmp`, `reset`, `fdt`
- Recipes: TFTP boot, flashing QSPI, loading a bitstream
